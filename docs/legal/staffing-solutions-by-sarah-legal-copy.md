## What was corrected

1. The earlier note saying a separate cookie notice is "mandatory under PIPEDA" was too broad.
   - Better: because the site uses analytics, the Privacy Policy must clearly disclose analytics/tracking practices. A banner or consent tool may also be appropriate depending on how the site is configured.

2. The earlier wording around Google Analytics needed tightening.
   - Better: Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to measure site usage.

3. The earlier wording around Vercel needed to be specific to Vercel Web Analytics.
   - Better: Vercel Web Analytics is described by Vercel as using anonymized data and not using cookies.

4. The CASL footer note alone is not enough for compliance.
   - Better: keep a short CASL note, but actual email practices must also respect consent and unsubscribe rules if commercial emails are ever sent.

5. The legal set for this site should be three pages:
   - Privacy Policy
   - Terms of Use
   - Recruitment Disclaimer

---

## Plain-English summary

### Privacy Policy
This tells people what information the business collects, why it collects it, how it uses it, and who to contact about it.

### Terms of Use
This sets the rules for using the website and helps limit business risk if someone relies on site content or misuses the site.

### Recruitment Disclaimer
This says the website does not promise a job, a placement, an interview, or a client engagement. Using the site does not create an employment relationship.

### Analytics
The site tracks usage. That needs to be disclosed clearly.

### CASL
If the business ever starts sending marketing emails or newsletters, it needs proper consent and unsubscribe handling.
